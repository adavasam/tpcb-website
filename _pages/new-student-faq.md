This page is for students who have already accepted an offer. It covers what has to happen between accepting and starting in the lab. The program office sends the forms, portals and logins themselves by email; what follows is the shape of each step and who owns it.

If something here does not match what you have been sent, the email is authoritative — write to [{{ site.data.program.contact_email }}](mailto:{{ site.data.program.contact_email }}) and ask.

## Health requirements

### What do I need to submit?

Weill Cornell requires health history information, documentation of required immunizations, and a completed physical exam. These go on the Student Health Packet, which the program provides.

Forms are due no later than **30 days before the program starts — June 1**. Completed forms must be **mailed**; they are not accepted by fax or email.

### Where do I find information on health insurance?

WCM health insurance is administered by Student Accounting. Their New Student Checklist is the thing to read first.

## Payroll and stipend

### When does the stipend start?

Before the program starts, a Human Resources staff member sends you new employment forms and schedules a virtual onboarding meeting. Once those forms clear, the stipend is paid every other Friday.

### When can I start spending my research allowance?

Once you are enrolled in WCM payroll. The research allowance policy is reissued each year, so use the current year's form rather than one you have seen before.

### What do international students need to do?

Two things beyond the standard onboarding:

- **Activate F-1 status.** All new and transfer international students complete an online check-in to activate F-1 status at Weill Cornell.
- **Tax questionnaire.** International students complete a tax questionnaire as part of HR onboarding. The HR Immigration team uses it to determine tax withholding status and will ask you to sign treaty exemption forms if any apply.

## Accounts and ID cards

### How do I activate my CWID?

Your CWID — Center-Wide ID, pronounced "seaweed" — is your Weill Cornell Graduate School user ID, and it is what logs you into WCM systems. Activate it once your personal credentials arrive.

### How do I check my Weill Cornell email?

Your address is your CWID followed by `@med.cornell.edu`. Outlook or the Office 365 web interface both work, from anywhere with an internet connection.

### How do I get on the Weill Cornell network?

Submit a new connection request through myHelpdesk, the self-service portal for IT issues and requests. You need an active CWID and password to sign in.

### How do I get a Weill Cornell ID card?

Once HR has processed your new employee forms, go to the NewYork-Presbyterian/Weill Cornell Security Office at **523 East 70th Street** (the "Annex" building). Tell the officer you are a new student, and bring a government photo ID.

### How do I get an ID for a rotation at Memorial Sloan Kettering?

Complete the MSK non-employee form and submit it to the MSK Office of Graduate Studies. Once your non-employee status is processed, MSK HR sends you a link to a non-employee survey to complete.

### How do I get an ID for a rotation at Rockefeller?

Email Kristen Cullen in the Dean's Office, copying Margie Hinonangan-Mendoza, the Tri-Institutional Program Administrator, with your name, program, rotation start and end dates, and the RU lab name. Once it is approved you will be directed to the Nurses Residence, first floor, for ID photos.

## Registering for classes

### Weill Cornell

Course listings live in LEARN and all registration happens there; an active CWID is required. Registration instructions go out by email before each registration and add/drop period. The Academic Calendar has the dates.

### Rockefeller University

Students may apply to take Rockefeller courses each semester by completing an application in LEARN.

### Columbia University

Registration for Columbia courses is handled separately, and students planning to take them enroll through the Columbia School of Professional Studies. Ask the program office for the current instructions.
