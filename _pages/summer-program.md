## The Chemical Biology Summer Program

The Tri-Institutional Chemical Biology Summer Program (ChBSP) is offered jointly by TPCB and the Office of Science Education & Training at Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center. It is a ten-week research experience for outstanding undergraduates in chemistry, biochemistry, and chemical biology who are interested in a career at the intersection of chemistry and biomedicine, hosted in labs on the Memorial Sloan Kettering, Rockefeller, and Weill Cornell campuses. Applicants must have chemistry coursework and laboratory experience.

ChBSP is strongly committed to promoting a supportive training environment in science. The program has zero tolerance for racism or discrimination in any form.

## What the summer looks like

Students who are accepted to ChBSP will:

- Adapt their chemistry skills to tackle cutting-edge problems in biomedical research
- Work in preeminent research labs at Memorial Sloan Kettering, Rockefeller, or Weill Cornell
- Interact with faculty, postdoctoral fellows, and graduate students
- Attend weekly scientific lectures by faculty
- Take part in weekly journal club discussions
- Attend professional development workshops
- Present their research in a poster session at the end of the summer

## Who can apply

Undergraduate freshmen, sophomores, and juniors majoring in chemistry, biochemistry, chemical biology, or a related field are invited to apply. Eligible applicants must be authorized to work in the US.

## How to apply

The application is completed and submitted online through Memorial Sloan Kettering. For the 2026 cycle, the portal opened on November 3, 2025 and the deadline was February 2, 2026 at 8:00 am ET.

Application requirements:

- An interest in applications of chemistry to biomedical problems
- A personal statement describing research background and interests
- Completion of college-level chemistry courses, with a minimum 3.0 GPA overall
- Two letters of recommendation from faculty members familiar with the applicant's academic preparation and research potential, submitted by those faculty members directly through the online system
- An unofficial transcript, uploaded into the application system. Only applicants who are accepted need to submit an official transcript, after acceptance. Questions about transcripts go to [oset@mskcc.org](mailto:oset@mskcc.org)

Offers of lab placement are made no later than March 15.
