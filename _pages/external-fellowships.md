Every TPCB student is supported by a fellowship from the program itself — see [Prospective Students]({{ '/prospective/' | relative_url }}) for what that covers. External fellowships are additional: they carry recognition, they give a student a funding record of their own, and each TPCB student is required to submit at least one external application during their studies.

TPCB students have competed successfully for the National Science Foundation (NSF) Graduate Research Fellowship, the National Institutes of Health (NIH) Ruth L. Kirschstein predoctoral fellowship, the Natural Sciences and Engineering Research Council of Canada (NSERC) graduate fellowship, and the Singapore Agency for Science, Technology and Research (A\*STAR) National Science Scholarship, among others.

The list below is the one the program maintains, ordered roughly by the stage at which you become eligible. Deadlines and terms change every cycle — check each sponsor's own site before you plan around one.

## Open to undergraduates and early graduate students

| Fellowship | Eligibility |
|---|---|
| National Science Foundation (NSF) Graduate Research Fellowship Program (GRFP) | Undergraduate or graduate student (1st year) |
| National Defense Science & Engineering Graduate (NDSEG) Fellowship | Undergraduate or graduate student (1st or 2nd year) |
| Howard Hughes Medical Institute (HHMI) Gilliam Fellowships for Advanced Study | Undergraduate or graduate student |
| The Hertz Foundation Graduate Fellowship | Undergraduate or graduate student |
| Ford Foundation Predoctoral Fellowships | Undergraduate or graduate student |
| Josephine de Karman Fellowship Trust | Undergraduate or graduate student, in the last 18 months of study |
| Paul & Daisy Soros Fellowships for New Americans | Undergraduate or graduate student (1st–2nd year); specific citizenship requirements |
| Natural Sciences and Engineering Research Council of Canada (NSERC) | Undergraduate or graduate student (1st–3rd year); must be a Canadian citizen |

| Canadian Institutes of Health Research (CIHR) Doctoral Foreign Study Award (DFSA) | Graduate student; must be a Canadian citizen or permanent resident |

## Getting help with an application

A fellowships workshop is part of the first-year July curriculum, and the external fellowship application most students write builds on the thesis research proposal they defend at the end of Year 2 — see [Program of Study]({{ '/program-of-study/' | relative_url }}).
