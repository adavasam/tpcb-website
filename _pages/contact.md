## Finding us

The Tri-Institutional campuses meet at East 68th Street and York Avenue, on the Upper East Side of Manhattan. The nearest subway stations are all within walking distance:

- **Q** — 72nd Street & Second Avenue
- **6** — 68th Street–Hunter College
- **F** — Lexington Avenue–63rd Street

The campus is served by all three New York airports and by New York Penn Station.

### From New York Penn Station

By taxi, 20–30 minutes at the metered rate.

By subway, 25–35 minutes: walk east one block to 34th Street–Herald Square, take the Second Avenue-bound Q to 72nd Street, leave by the south end of the platform at 69th Street & Second Avenue, then walk east and south to 68th Street & York Avenue.

### From LaGuardia Airport (LGA)

By taxi, 20–30 minutes at the metered rate, plus tolls.

By bus and subway, 70–80 minutes: the M60 bus to 125th Street & Lexington Avenue, then the downtown 6 to 68th Street–Hunter College, then walk east to York Avenue.

### From John F. Kennedy Airport (JFK)

By taxi, 40–50 minutes at the Manhattan flat rate, plus tolls.

By train and subway, 75–90 minutes: the JFK AirTrain to Jamaica Station, the Manhattan-bound E to Lexington Avenue–53rd Street, then the Bronx-bound 6 to 68th Street–Hunter College, then walk east to York Avenue.

### From Newark Liberty Airport (EWR)

By taxi, 50–60 minutes at the metered rate, plus surcharges and tolls.

By train and subway, 70–90 minutes: the EWR AirTrain to Newark Airport Station, NJ Transit to New York Penn Station, the Queens-bound E to Lexington Avenue–53rd Street, then the Bronx-bound 6 to 68th Street–Hunter College, then walk east to York Avenue.

Taxi fares, transit fares and airport flat rates all change; check the MTA, the NYC Taxi and Limousine Commission or NJ Transit for current prices before you travel.
