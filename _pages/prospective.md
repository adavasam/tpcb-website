## How to apply

Applications are submitted through the [TPCB application portal]({{ site.data.program.application_url }}). The online application, **including receipt of letters of recommendation**, is due **{{ site.data.program.application_deadline }}**.

The portal lists the current requirements and supporting materials — check it rather than this page for the definitive list, since requirements are set each cycle.

Application fee waivers are provided proactively to students recruited through pipeline programs. Students who matriculate are reimbursed for the cost of official transcripts and score reports.

## The Open House

Invited applicants come to New York in January for the Interview and Recruiting Open House. It runs across the three campuses and typically includes faculty research presentations, a student poster session, a session on student life and housing, an overview of the core facilities, an alumni panel, and time with the Student Organizing Committee.

Candidates receive their individual schedule, a campus map and the shared Open House materials by email, along with an invitation to the program's Slack channel. Travel to the Open House is reimbursed; reimbursement forms are distributed with the Open House materials.

## Funding and support

Every TPCB student receives a graduate fellowship. It covers:

- a stipend, paid for the duration of the PhD
- tuition and fees
- subsidized housing on or near campus, on the Upper East Side
- health insurance, for students and their dependent children
- an annual research allowance — conference travel, a laptop or tablet, scientific books and other research resources

First-year students receive additional funds toward relocation expenses and official transcript fees.

Students are not responsible for securing their own funding, but every TPCB student is required to submit at least one external fellowship application during their studies. See [External Fellowships]({{ '/external-fellowships/' | relative_url }}) for the list the program maintains.

## Common questions

### How many rotations are there, and where?

At least three, during the first year, and they must span at least two of the three campuses — rotating entirely within one institution is not an option. Students select a thesis laboratory by the end of Year 1. Rotation lengths and dates are on the [Program of Study]({{ '/program-of-study/' | relative_url }}) page.

### How long does the PhD take?

TPCB sets no fixed graduation deadline. Most students finish in about five and a half years, close to the national average.

### Which institution grants the degree?

Students receive their PhD from the institution where they carry out their thesis research — except students in Sloan Kettering laboratories, who receive their degree from Weill Cornell.

### Do I need to bring my own funding?

No. Fellowship support comes from the program. External fellowships are encouraged and one application is required during training, but admission and funding do not depend on them.
