TPCB faculty member **Prof. Vanessa Ruta** has been named an Investigator of the Howard Hughes Medical Institute. She is one of 33 new HHMI Investigators selected this year, each of whom will receive approximately $9M in research funding over a 7-year term. Prof. Ruta’s lab at Rockefeller studies the molecular and neural mechanisms of olfaction in *Drosophila melanogaster*, exploring both the structural basis of odor detection at the receptor level and neural algorithms of odor navigation at the behavioral level. She joins six other current HHMI Investigators on the TPCB faculty.
