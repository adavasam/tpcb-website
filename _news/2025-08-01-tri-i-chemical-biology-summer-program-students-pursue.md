The **Tri-Institutional Chemical Biology Summer Program (ChBSP)** provides undergraduate students with opportunities to pursue forefront research in chemical biology through summer internships in TPCB labs. The program hosted 5 outstanding students this year carried out hands-on research projects with our faculty and students, attended scientific and career development presentations, participated in a journal club hosted by TPCB students, and enjoyed outings in NYC. The students presented at a poster session with other summer interns on August 7, 2025 on the campus of MSK.

Since its inception in 2018, ChBSP has been directed by **Prof. Daniel Bachovchin**, a TPCB faculty member, with support from the MSK Office of Scientific Education and Training. This year, the program received generous financial support from the Weill Cornell Graduate School, the MSK Center for Experimental Therapeutics, the MSK Office of Scientific Education and Training, the Rockefeller University Pels Center, and the David Rockefeller Graduate School. The ChBSP journal club was hosted by TPCB students **Charles Warren** (himself a ChBSP alum) and **Raymond Lam**, with **Anoosha Banerjee**, **Michelle Guo**, **Atara Neugroschl** (also a ChBSP alum), **Shuyao Su**, and **Jaina Wollowitz**.

**Katrina Diaz**, University of Pittsburgh at Johnstown, Class of 2026

*NLRP3 Inflammasome Regulation by NEK7 and NEK9*, with Prof. Daniel Bachovchin, MSK

**Aliya Fisher**, Stanford University, Class of 2026

*Developing Covalent Inhibitors of HCV Helicase Using Electrophilic Scout Fragments*, with Prof. Tarun Kapoor, Rockefeller

**Amanda Pang**, University of Pennsylvania, Class of 2026

*Mechanistic and Structural Insight into Heptatis B Virus Polymerase*, with Prof. Seth Darst, Rockefeller

**Lauren Printz**, Stonehill College, Class of 2026

*The Role of Mitochondrial Proteins CHCHD10 abnd CHCHD2 in Neurodegenerative Disease Progression*, with Prof. David Eliezer, Weill Cornell

**Isaac Puckett**, Middle Tennessee State University, Class of 2026

*Design and Synthesis of a Novel Furan-based γ-Secretase Modulator for Alzheimer’s Disease*, with Prof. Yueming Li, MSK
