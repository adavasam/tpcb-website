TPCB faculty member **Prof. Christopher D. Lima** of Sloan-Kettering’s Structural Biology Program has been selected as a Howard Hughes Medical Institute Investigator. He is one of 27 selected in the recent competition from more than 1,200 applicants. Dr. Lima’s laboratory uses structural, biochemical, and functional analyses of macromolecules to understand RNA metabolism and its role in cell growth and disease. Dr. Lima joins TPCB’s three other HHMI investigators: Jonathan Goldberg, Roderick MacKinnon, and Nikola Pavletich, along with HHMI Early Career Scientist Sean Brady.
