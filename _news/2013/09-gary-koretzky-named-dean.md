tags: [students]

One of the world’s leading immunologists, **Gary Koretzky, MD, PhD**, has been named Dean of the Weill Cornell Graduate School of Medical Sciences and Senior Associate Dean for Research at Weill Cornell Medical College, effective October 1, 2013. He succeeds Dr. David Hajjar and joins Dean Sidney Strickland of Rockefeller University and Dr. Thomas Kelly of Memorial Sloan-Kettering Cancer Center on the Executive Committee of TPCB. Dr. Koretzky has been the Francis C. Wood Professor of Medicine at the University of Pennsylvania School of Medicine and studies the biochemistry of T lymphocyte development. He is a member of the Institute of Medicine and a Fellow of the American Academy of Arts and Sciences.
