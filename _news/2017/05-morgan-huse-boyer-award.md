**Prof. Morgan Huse** at Sloan Kettering has been honored with the Louise and Alston Boyer Young Investigator Award for Excellence in Basic Research, which recognizes outstanding junior faculty at MSK. Prof. Huse’s lab combines imaging technology with synthetic chemistry and materials science to understand how immune cells communicate. He joins several other TPCB faculty members who received this honor in recent years: Christopher Lima (2006), Stephen Long (2016), Derek Tan and (2010).
