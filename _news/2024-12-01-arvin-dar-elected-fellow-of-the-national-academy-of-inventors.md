TPCB faculty member **Prof. Arvin Dar** at Sloan Kettering has been elected as a Fellow of the National Academy of Inventors. Election as a Fellow is the highest professional distinction awarded solely to inventors, recognizing individuals whose patents and discoveries have made a tangible impact on quality of life, economic development, and the welfare of society. Prof. Dar joins this distinguished group in recognition of his seminal contributions to small-molecule drug discovery in cancer. His lab develops chemical tools and probes to dissect signal transduction pathways that drive cancer, with a particular focus on the Ras-MAPK signaling pathway, which is activated in over 20% of human cancers. Among the lab’s key inventions are small molecules that lock the kinase suppressor of Ras (KSR) in an inactive conformation, thus blocking oncogenic Ras-MAPK signaling. This discovery revealed the structural basis for how clinically used MEK inhibitors interact with KSR, providing a blueprint for designing more selective and effective cancer drugs in the future. Prof. Dar is the first TPCB faculty member to be elected to the National Academy of Inventors.
