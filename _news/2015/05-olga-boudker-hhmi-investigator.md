TPCB faculty member **Prof. Olga Boudker** of Weill Cornell Medical College has been selected as a Howard Hughes Medical Institute Investigator. She is one of 26 selected in the recent competition from nearly 900 applicants. Prof. Boudker’s laboratory uses structural, biophysical, and protein engineering techniques to understanding the mechanisms of membrane transporters. She joins TPCB’s other five HHMI investigators: Jue Chen, Jonathan Goldberg, Christopher Lima, Roderick MacKinnon, and Nikola Pavletich.
