**Prof. Sebastian Klinge** at Rockefeller has been awarded a prestigious Director’s New Innovator grant by the NIH. This $1.5 million, five-year grant will support his lab’s research into assembly of the eukaryotic ribosome. Ribosomes are essential molecular machines that catalyze protein synthesis in cells. The lab’s research focuses on elucidating both the structural anatomy of the ribosome assembly machinery as well as the underlying biochemical transformations involved in the assembly process. Prof. Klinge joined the Rockefeller faculty as an Assistant Professor in 2013 and joined the TPCB faculty shortly thereafter. He is the seventh TPCB faculty member to have received NIH Director’s New Innovator Award since the program’s inception in 2007.
