TPCB was proud to co-sponsor the New York Academy of Sciences event, **Click Chemistry in Biology and Medicine Symposium: New Developments and Strategies**. The symposium was held on **September 15, 2014** and explored recent developments in click chemistry, including the discovery of new click reactions. The program included keynote talks by 2001 Nobel Laureate Prof. K. Barry Sharpless and Prof. James Paulson, both of The Scripps Research Institute. Other speakers included Prof. Yimon Aye (Cornell University), Prof. David A. Spiegel (Yale University) and Prof. Peng Wu (Albert Einstein College of Medicine). Read more at: [NYAS](http://www.nyas.org/Events/Detail.aspx?cid=07d3f871-9269-4f16-ae8f-482d20e7450d)
