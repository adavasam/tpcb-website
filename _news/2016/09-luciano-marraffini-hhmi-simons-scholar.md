TPCB faculty member **Prof. Luciano Marraffini** at Rockefeller has been named a Howard Hughes Medical Institute Faculty Scholar. This new program supports early-career scientists who have great potential to make unique contributions to their fields and was established recently by HHMI, The Simons Foundation, and The Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation. Prof. Marraffini’s lab studies the fundamental mechanisms behind CRISPR-based immunity in bacteria and other microorganisms. The system enables the bacteria to remember the DNA sequences of invading phages, so they can cleave viral DNA upon subsequent infections. In 2008, while a postdoc at Northwestern University, Dr. Marraffini demonstrated that CRISPR immunity targets the DNA of the invader, rather than the RNA as previously thought. More recently, his lab at Rockefeller has gained important insights into the roles of the enzyme Cas9 in acquisition and implementation of this immunity, as well as our understanding of how bacteria distinguish between harmful and beneficial viruses. Prof. Marraffini joined the Rockefeller faculty in 2010 and was promoted to Associate Professor earlier this year.
