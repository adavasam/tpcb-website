**Dr. Harold Varmus**, co-founder of the TPCB program, joins Weill Cornell Medical College’s faculty as the Lewis Thomas University Professor of Medicine and will collaborate with investigators at the Sandra and Edward Meyer Cancer Center, led by Meyer Director and TPCB faculty member Prof. Lewis C. Cantley. Dr. Varmus was the President of the Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center from 2000–2010 and was instrumental in the founding of the Tri-Institutional Research Program. He most recently Director of the National Cancer Institute until March 2015. Dr. Varmus was awarded the Nobel Prize in Medicine with J. Michael Bishop in 1989 for the discovery of the cellular origin of retroviral oncogenes. Read more at: [Weill Cornell News](http://weill.cornell.edu/news/pr/2015/03/nobel-laureate-harold-varmus-joins-weill-cornell-medical-college-to-advance-cancer-research.html)
