TPCB is pleased to announce that **Clare Cahir** has been honored as recipient of this year’s **TPCB Student Service Award**. This award recognizes a current TPCB student who has distinguished themself in service to the program, the Tri-I community, and/or the broader scientific community.

Clare has been an outstanding leader in the TPCB and Tri-I community. In particular, she has played a major role in the High School Catalyst Program at Weill Cornell, which provides hands-on laboratory research experiences for aspiring young scientists. She has served as an Advisor since 2021 and also as Co-President in 2023–2024, when she secured a grant to provide stipend support for the trainees. Clare has also served as a mentor for four years in the Tri-I Mentorship Initiative (TIMI), working one-on-one with students applying to graduate school in the biomedical sciences. She also led the Women in Science at Rockefeller University (WISeR) group for two years, instituting a biannual lecture series and hosting networking events for women scientists. Most recently, Clare has collaborated with the RockEDU science outreach program to develop a new course entitled “America’s Next Top Model Organism” to educate high school students about various model organisms used to study biology and disease. Within TPCB, Clare also led the TPCB Student Programming Committee in 2021–2023 and co-organized our first post-pandemic TPCB Student Career Development Retreat in 2022.

Recent TPCB graduate **Dr. Gianna Stella** commented, “It is in Clare’s nature to create positive change wherever she is and she has an amazing capacity for serving others. She has a genuine passion and exuberance for outreach and mentoring and seeks to positively impact the world around her.”

In addition to her exceptional contributions to these programs, Clare is also an outstanding scientist whose research focuses on understanding, on a molecular level, CRISPR-Cas systems in bacterial immunity and how these CRISPR systems can be thwarted by competing mechanisms. She is currently a senior graduate student working with **Prof. Luciano Marraffini** at Rockefeller, and she plans to pursue a position in biotech after graduation.

The TPCB Student Service Awardee is selected each year by a committee of TPCB students and faculty, chaired by **Prof. Daniel Heller** at MSK, based on nominations solicited across the program each spring. Clare was presented with the award at the annual TPCB First-Year Welcome and Student Service Award Ceremony, which was held on the campus of Rockefeller University on July 28, 2025, and organized by TPCB students **Antonio Collado**, **Raymond Lam**, **Ariel Pan**, **Mihika Shah**, and **Shuyao Su**.
