The Yueming Li laboratory investigates the biology and pharmacology of intramembrane proteases—a class of enzymes that cleave their substrates within the hydrophobic core of the lipid bilayer. A primary focus is gamma-secretase, a multi-subunit intramembrane protease that cleaves the amyloid precursor protein (APP) to generate amyloid-beta peptides central to Alzheimer's disease, and also processes Notch receptors to regulate cell fate decisions in development and cancer. The lab uses chemical proteomics, structural biology, and cell biology to understand the molecular mechanism of gamma-secretase substrate selection and to develop selective modulators that shift cleavage toward less amyloidogenic products. Additional work investigates other intramembrane proteases and their roles in pathogen infection and inflammatory signaling.
