The Jue Chen laboratory investigates the structure, mechanism, and regulation of ATP-binding cassette (ABC) transporters, a superfamily of ATP-powered pumps that move substrates across cellular membranes with profound implications for multidrug resistance and human disease. Using cryo-electron microscopy and X-ray crystallography, the lab captures these molecular machines in multiple states along their catalytic cycles, illuminating how ATP binding and hydrolysis drive conformational changes that power substrate translocation. Current projects include defining the structural basis of drug efflux by bacterial and human ABC transporters, understanding how lipid composition and membrane environment modulate transporter activity, and elucidating the mechanisms of clinically important human transporters implicated in cystic fibrosis, adrenal insufficiency, and cancer chemoresistance.
