The Jiang laboratory investigates the molecular mechanisms of cell death—particularly apoptosis, ferroptosis, and autophagy—and how these pathways can be harnessed to eliminate cancer cells. A primary focus is ferroptosis, a form of regulated cell death driven by iron-dependent lipid peroxidation; the lab has made important contributions to understanding the execution mechanisms of ferroptosis and identifying key regulators such as GPX4 and the FSP1-CoQ10 axis. The group also studies how autophagy—a cellular self-digestion pathway—regulates tumor cell survival under nutrient deprivation and chemotherapy stress, and how cells acclimated to low-oxygen environments develop resistance to ferroptosis. Chemical biology approaches, including CRISPR screens and compound library screening in death pathway assays, are used to discover genes and small molecules that modulate cell death sensitivity in cancer.
