The Keshari laboratory develops novel molecular imaging agents and metabolic probes to non-invasively map cancer metabolism in patients and animal models. A flagship technology is hyperpolarized 13C magnetic resonance spectroscopic imaging (MRSI), which amplifies the NMR signal of 13C-labeled substrates like pyruvate by orders of magnitude, enabling real-time measurement of metabolic flux in vivo. The lab designs hyperpolarized probes for key metabolic reactions—lactate production (Warburg effect), lipid synthesis, pentose phosphate pathway activity—and applies them to characterize tumor metabolism, monitor treatment response, and identify metabolic biomarkers. Additional research programs investigate metabolic reprogramming in immune cells for cancer immunotherapy and develop imaging approaches for brain tumor diagnosis. Dr. Keshari holds the Fred Lebow Chair and directs the Center for Molecular Imaging and Bioengineering at MSK.
