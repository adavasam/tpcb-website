The Wilkinson laboratory investigates the biology of bacterial non-coding RNAs and RNA-based molecular machines, with a particular focus on reverse transcriptases and retrotransposons. Bacteria encode diverse RNA-dependent enzymes and regulatory elements that remain largely uncharacterized but play important roles in gene regulation, phage defense, and antibiotic resistance. The lab uses structural biology (cryo-EM, X-ray crystallography), biochemistry, bioinformatics, and bacterial genetics to characterize novel RNA enzymes and RNA-protein complexes in diverse bacteria. A key goal is discovering new reverse transcriptase classes and RNA regulatory mechanisms in environmental microbiomes, expanding the known RNA mechanistic repertoire. These fundamental discoveries may reveal new antibiotic targets in bacterial RNA metabolism.
