The Luo laboratory develops chemical biology tools and drug candidates targeting protein methyltransferases, which catalyze the transfer of methyl groups to histone lysines and arginines as well as to other protein substrates. Protein methylation is a major epigenetic regulatory mechanism, and protein lysine methyltransferases (PKMTs) and protein arginine methyltransferases (PRMTs) are frequently overexpressed or mutated in cancer. The lab uses mechanism-based enzyme inhibitors, bisubstrate analogs, and SAM cofactor analogs to develop selective chemical probes for specific methyltransferases, and couples these with proteomics to identify substrates and physiological roles. Several inhibitors developed by the Luo lab are in preclinical or clinical evaluation as potential cancer therapeutics.
