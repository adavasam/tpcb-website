The Gudas laboratory investigates the pharmacology of retinoids—vitamin A and its derivatives—and their mechanisms of action in differentiation, cancer prevention, and treatment. Retinoids exert profound effects on cell fate through nuclear retinoic acid receptors (RARs) and retinoid X receptors (RXRs) that regulate gene expression programs controlling differentiation and proliferation. The lab uses mouse genetic models, epigenomic profiling, and pharmacological approaches to understand how retinoic acid signaling is dysregulated in various cancers—including renal carcinoma, lung cancer, and leukemia—and to identify retinoid-based therapeutic strategies. Additional work focuses on how epigenetic mechanisms, including DNA methylation and histone modifications, interact with retinoid signaling to determine cell identity and cancer susceptibility.
