The Vinogradova laboratory develops and applies chemical proteomic technologies to understand and manipulate immune cell signaling. The immune system employs an enormous number of proteins—many of which lack known functions or selective chemical probes—that collectively orchestrate host defense, inflammation, and immune tolerance. The lab uses activity-based protein profiling (ABPP) and covalent ligand discovery platforms to systematically map the reactive cysteome of immune cells, identify new druggable sites on immune proteins, and develop selective covalent probes to study and modulate those proteins in living cells. These chemical tools illuminate how post-translational modifications and metabolite-derived electrophiles regulate immune function, and provide starting points for new immunomodulatory therapeutics.
