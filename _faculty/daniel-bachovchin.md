The Bachovchin laboratory develops and deploys chemical probes to discover the functions of uncharacterized enzymes and protein-protein interactions, with a focus on the innate immune system and cancer biology. The group uses activity-based protein profiling (ABPP) with reactive serine hydrolase and cysteine protease probes to identify enzymatic activities whose functions remain unknown, then characterizes their mechanisms and biological roles. Recent work has illuminated new catalytic activities in the cGAS-STING and NLRP3 inflammasome pathways that regulate innate immune sensing. The lab also develops substrate-specific probes targeting caspases and other proteases, and screens chemical libraries in phenotypic assays to find inhibitors that modulate inflammasome activation—a target of intense interest for inflammatory diseases, autoimmunity, and cancer immunotherapy.
