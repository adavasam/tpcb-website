The Stephen Long laboratory uses X-ray crystallography and cryo-electron microscopy to determine high-resolution structures of ion channels and membrane-embedded enzymes, with the goal of understanding the molecular mechanisms by which these proteins function and can be pharmacologically modulated. The lab has made foundational contributions to the structural understanding of voltage-gated potassium channels and calcium channels, revealing how voltage-sensing domains couple electrochemical gradients to gate opening. Current research encompasses structurally diverse membrane proteins including lipid-metabolizing enzymes embedded in the membrane, and studies the mechanisms of both channel and non-channel membrane proteins that are important drug targets for cardiovascular disease, pain, and cancer.
