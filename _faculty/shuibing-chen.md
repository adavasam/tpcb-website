The Shuibing Chen laboratory applies chemical biology approaches to human pluripotent stem cell biology, with a focus on directing stem cell differentiation toward medically important cell types and identifying small molecules that expand or reprogram cell fate. The group screens chemical libraries in stem cell-derived models to discover compounds that promote the generation of pancreatic beta cells (for diabetes therapy), intestinal organoids (for studying gut disease), and neural cell types (for modeling neurological conditions). A complementary direction uses stem cell platforms to model COVID-19 and identify host factors and antivirals. The lab exemplifies a chemical biology strategy in which small molecules serve both as mechanistic probes—revealing the pathways that control cell identity—and as the basis for new regenerative medicine and antiviral therapies.
