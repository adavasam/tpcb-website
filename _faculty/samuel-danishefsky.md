The Danishefsky laboratory is renowned for the total synthesis of complex natural products, with particular emphasis on antitumor and anti-infective agents. Professor Danishefsky and his group have developed enabling synthetic methodologies—including the Danishefsky diene for [4+2] cycloaddition reactions—that have enabled the synthesis of compounds previously considered beyond reach, including epothilone, taxol, cycloprodiginine, and numerous other biologically important natural products. Beyond synthesis, the lab pioneered the use of synthetic carbohydrate chemistry to create fully synthetic cancer vaccines incorporating tumor-associated carbohydrate antigens (TACAs) conjugated to carrier proteins, generating humoral immune responses against cancer. This translational program has advanced candidates toward clinical investigation.
