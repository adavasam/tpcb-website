The Menon laboratory investigates the molecular mechanisms that maintain membrane lipid asymmetry and direct the trafficking of lipids and lipid-modified proteins in eukaryotic cells. Cellular membranes are not uniform bilayers; specific lipid species are asymmetrically distributed between the two leaflets of the membrane and among different organelles, and disruption of this organization underlies diseases including cancer and neurodegeneration. The lab uses biochemical reconstitution, cell biology, and yeast genetics to characterize the scramblases, flippases, and lipid transfer proteins that establish and maintain lipid asymmetry. Complementary work investigates how proteins are modified with GPI anchors and fatty acids at the ER and how these modifications direct protein sorting to the plasma membrane and lipid raft microdomains.
