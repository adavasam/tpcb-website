The Hite laboratory determines cryo-electron microscopy structures of ion channels, metabolite transporters, and related membrane proteins to understand the molecular mechanisms of ion and metabolite transport and its links to genomic integrity. The lab has determined landmark structures of voltage-gated calcium channels, IP3 receptors, and lipid-linked metabolite channels such as FLVCR1, revealing how these proteins select among substrates, gate in response to diverse stimuli, and interact with pharmacological modulators. A key research direction investigates how the transport of specific lipid metabolites influences membrane composition and organelle function, with implications for understanding metabolic disease, neurodegeneration, and cancer biology. A second research direction studies protein–nucleic acid interactions critical for maintaining genomic integrity, using cryo-EM, electrophysiology, and cell biological approaches.
