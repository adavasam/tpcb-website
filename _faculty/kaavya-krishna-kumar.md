The Krishna Kumar laboratory investigates the structural and molecular mechanisms underlying cellular signaling through G protein-coupled receptors (GPCRs), with a particular focus on how these receptors recognize their ligands, couple to intracellular signaling partners, and are regulated. GPCRs constitute the largest family of cell-surface receptors and are the targets of approximately 35% of approved drugs. The lab uses cryo-electron microscopy, pharmacological assays, and cell-based functional studies to characterize receptor conformational states, map allosteric sites, and understand the structural basis of biased agonism—the ability of certain ligands to selectively engage subsets of downstream signaling pathways. These structural insights aim to guide the design of more selective and safer drugs targeting GPCRs in neuroscience, endocrinology, and oncology.
