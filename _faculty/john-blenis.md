The Blenis laboratory investigates signal transduction pathways that control cell growth, proliferation, metabolism, and survival, with a focus on how these pathways are hijacked in cancer to drive tumorigenesis and drug resistance. A core research theme is the PI3K-mTOR-S6 kinase axis: the lab has made fundamental contributions to understanding how mTOR complexes integrate nutrient, growth factor, and energy signals to control anabolic metabolism and ribosome biogenesis. Additional work explores the links between the PI3K pathway and cancer metabolism—particularly how metabolic reprogramming in tumor cells facilitates metastasis and therapy resistance. The group uses proteomics, metabolomics, mouse tumor models, and pharmacological approaches to identify vulnerabilities in oncogenic signaling networks and to translate these findings toward improved cancer treatments.
