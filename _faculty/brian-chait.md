The Chait laboratory develops and applies mass spectrometry-based tools to tackle fundamental problems in structural and cell biology. A key research direction involves developing hybrid structural approaches—integrating mass spectrometry with cryo-electron microscopy and computational modeling—to determine the architecture of large, flexible macromolecular machines that are difficult to study by single techniques alone. The lab has made seminal contributions to the structural characterization of the nuclear pore complex (in collaboration with the Rout lab), the RNAP transcription machinery, and diverse protein complexes relevant to cancer and infectious disease. Method development in the group spans new ionization strategies, cross-linking mass spectrometry, and quantitative proteomics to map protein interactions and modifications in vivo.
