The Lyu laboratory harnesses computational methods to discover novel small molecules that modulate difficult-to-drug biological targets. A central technology is large-scale molecular docking against ultra-large chemical libraries—enabled by algorithmic advances the group has pioneered—to efficiently identify potent and selective lead compounds without exhaustive experimental screening. The lab couples these computational predictions with structural biology (cryo-EM and X-ray crystallography) and medicinal chemistry to rapidly validate and optimize hits. Active research areas include developing small-molecule agonists and antagonists of GPCRs, ion channels, and other membrane proteins that have historically resisted drug discovery, with the ultimate goal of delivering preclinical candidates for neurological and oncological indications.
