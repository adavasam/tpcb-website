Three institutions, one program

TPCB students are admitted to a single program and may join a lab at any of its three institutions, all within a few blocks of each other on the Upper East Side. Each brings a different centre of gravity.

A single cohort across three campuses

Students from all three institutions take the same core curriculum, attend the same retreats and seminars, and rotate across campuses before choosing a thesis lab. The program is small enough that everyone knows everyone, and large enough to reach almost any area of chemical biology.
